Production Process

How We Work

Every project follows a structured production workflow, with clear communication and a practical project review before manufacturing begins.

  1. 01

    Initial Inquiry

    The client shares project information by email, WhatsApp or the contact form.

  2. 02

    Project Review

    Product type, estimated quantity, materials and production requirements are reviewed.

  3. 03

    Clarification

    Any missing technical details are discussed before production planning begins.

  4. 04

    Preliminary Assessment

    Production feasibility, sample requirements and the expected timeline are evaluated.

  5. 05

    Sample Development

    A sample is prepared after all production details have been confirmed.

  6. 06

    Approval & Quotation

    Pricing, production quantities and project expectations are confirmed.

  7. 07

    Production Planning

    Materials, workflow and quality checkpoints are organized.

  8. 08

    Production

    Manufacturing begins while communication continues throughout the project.

  9. 09

    Final Inspection

    Products are checked, finished and prepared for packing.

  10. 10

    Delivery Coordination

    Delivery arrangements are confirmed according to the agreed project requirements.

Quality Control

Quality Throughout the Process

Quality is monitored throughout production, not only after the final garment has been completed.

Material Inspection

Fabric quality, color consistency, accessories and visible defects are reviewed before use.

Cutting Inspection

Pattern accuracy, fabric direction, symmetry and cutting precision are checked before sewing.

In-Line Sewing Control

Stitch quality, seam consistency, clean finishing and construction accuracy are monitored during sewing.

Measurement Verification

Measurements are checked against approved specifications for size consistency and agreed tolerances.

Final Garment Inspection

Threads, stains, pressing, labels and the overall garment finish are reviewed.

Packing & Quantity Check

Models, sizes, colors and quantities are checked before carton preparation.

Products requiring correction are separated before final packing so that agreed quality expectations can be maintained throughout the production process.
